# Text: article, README, release notes, email

Pick 3 to 6. Each line is what the reader gets. Adjust the wording to your document.

- facts: every number, name, date and command matches the source it comes from (link, file, changelog)
- promise: the title and the first paragraph promise what the text delivers, nothing more
- complete: every point the brief asked for is present
- plain: a reader outside the team understands each sentence in one pass; every acronym is spelled out at first use
- action: the reader knows what to do next after the last paragraph
- no-filler: no sentence can be removed without losing a fact or a step

Not criteria (they belong in `optional`): "shorter", "more engaging", "I would open differently".
